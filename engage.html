Partner with Us

How learning happens matters as important to design as what is learned. To cultivate the capacities needed to intervene in complex systems, we must begin by shaping the right conditions for learning. We can help with that process through curriculum development, professional development, and strategic design. The groups we work in our lifelong learning ecosystem are arranged by how directly they engage with learners — from formal institutions and alternative learning spaces, through enterprises, to the civic and governance actors that shape the wider system.
