Engage Us

This framework adapts to many kinds of learning contexts. However you work with young people and systems change, we can help you put it into practice. Here's how different partners engage with us.

Formal Education Institutions

Schools and universities with progressive, interdisciplinary, or mission-driven curricula can use this framework to embed systems change competencies. It can shape capstone projects, transdisciplinary modules, teacher development, and student portfolios — offering a consistent reference point across academic and co-curricular learning.

Non-Formal Learning Spaces

Organizations running youth fellowships, innovation hubs, seasonal schools, or intergenerational learning programs can use the framework to scaffold experiential learning and systems thinking. Whether through residencies focused on community-based interventions or mentorship programs exploring climate justice, it helps clarify learning goals, design reflective activities, and articulate progression.

Civic & Governance Meta-Actors

Curriculum boards, philanthropic foundations, and policy think tanks can use this framework to guide educational funding strategies, influence policy priorities, or develop holistic assessment tools.

Social Enterprises & Capacity-Building Programs

Organizations offering internships, trainee programs, or early-career pathways — especially those focused on social innovation, design, or sustainability — can integrate the framework into onboarding, mentorship, and progression planning. It can inform personal development goals and support reflective practice across diverse roles.
