Learning to intervene in complex systems

A guided learning map for just & regenerative futures

As movements for justice and regeneration collide with systemic crises, navigating complexity requires a lifelong learning journey. Grounded in the question, What do we need to learn to intervene in complex systems, this open-source framework provides learning goals across 24 islands of coherence, a meta-process, and a resource library that can be applied in multiple contexts. It is designed for changemakers, educators, and leaders seeking an accessible, localized pathway into systemic intervention as a lifelong practice.
