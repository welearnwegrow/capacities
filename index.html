Learning to intervene in complex systems

A capacity framework for just & regenerative futures

We are living in an age where movements for justice and regeneration are colliding with ecological upheaval, widening inequality, technological disruption, and political fragmentation. This work asks one simple question: What do we need to learn to intervene in complex systems.  It outlines the orientations, capacities, and meta-process to navigate complexity and act with integrity in shaping better futures.
