Learning to intervene in complex systems

systems change practice x lifelong learning for just & regenerative futures

Everyday, we adapt to the systems around us, trying hard to change them towards more preferred futures. We believe that systems change work is a lifelong endeavor, with each season of our lives presenting new challenges that require different skills and capacities. This open-source framework starts with a single inquiry: What do we need to learn to intervene in complex systems? We landed at this map to guide changemakers, educators, and leaders ready for systems work. It provides orientations to ground the work in shared values, 24 learning areas, a process for intervening in complex systems, and a resource library from a community of system change practitioners and educators.
