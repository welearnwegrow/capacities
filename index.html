What do we need to learn to intervene in complex systems?

I need a method Use our design canvas → Five stages for intervening in complex systems, each with a reflection canvas you can download and work through. I need resources Browse the library → Books, toolkits, courses and cases gathered by a community of changemakers and educators. I need guidance Talk to us → A custom pathway designed with your team, on a sliding scale so budget is never the barrier.

Every day, we adapt to the systems around us while trying to change them towards preferred futures. Most of us learn how to do this as we go. We see systems change as a lifelong endeavor, and each season of our lives brings challenges that call for skills we have not needed before. This open-source guide gathers what we and a community of practitioners have learned into one map: 21 learning areas, a five-stage route and the orientations that ground it, each linked to resources. Find the area where you feel stuck or curious, and begin there.
