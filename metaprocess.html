Route

The Route is our meta-process for intervening in complex systems — five phases (Probe, Sense, Design, Adapt, Integrate) rather than a linear trajectory. It is an emergent, iterative dance between deep reflection and decisive action. These phases serve as a navigation tool, not a checklist — practitioners often find themselves moving fluidly between these phases as the system responds to their interventions and new insights reshape the problem field. Pinch or use the + / − buttons to zoom, swipe to pan, and Tap a stage to open its reflection canvas — fill it in on screen and download your own copy, or just download a blank canvas to print and work through by hand.

While non-linear, this process requires a disciplined relationship with time. Systems change is characterized by long periods of gradual accumulation punctuated by moments of rapid transformation. Practitioners must balance the urgency of the issue with the patience required for systemic shifts, often employing elastic deadlines and creating internal buffers to accommodate the unpredictable feedback loops and stakeholder dynamics inherent to this work.
