Meta-process

Systems change is rarely a linear trajectory. It is an emergent, iterative dance between deep reflection and decisive action. The following stages serve as a navigation tool, not a checklist — practitioners often find themselves moving fluidly between these phases as the system responds to their interventions and new insights reshape the problem field. Drag the waypoints, route, and feedback loops to make the map your own, then Pinch or use the + / − buttons to zoom, swipe to pan, and tap a stage to open its starter reflection canvas.

While non-linear, this process requires a disciplined relationship with time. Systems change is characterized by long periods of gradual accumulation punctuated by moments of rapid transformation. Practitioners must balance the urgency of the issue with the patience required for systemic shifts, often employing elastic deadlines and creating internal buffers to accommodate the unpredictable feedback loops and stakeholder dynamics inherent to this work.
