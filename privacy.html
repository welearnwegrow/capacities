Privacy Policy

Effective 1 July 2026

This website is maintained by Jaya Ramchandani & Raisa Mirza ("we", "us", "our"). We care about the small amount of personal information the site handles, and this policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We keep the site's data footprint deliberately light.

Information we collect

We only collect information you actively give us, plus basic anonymous usage data:

  • Newsletter sign-up. When you subscribe, we collect your email address so we can send you our newsletter.
  • Forms. If you use the "Partner with Us" contact form or suggest a resource, we collect what you enter — such as your name, email, organization, and message.
  • Usage analytics. Like most websites, we use a web analytics service to understand aggregate, anonymized traffic — pages visited, general location, device and browser type. This helps us improve the site. It is not used to identify you personally.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

How your information is used

  • To send you our newsletter, if you asked to receive it.
  • To read and respond to messages and resource suggestions you send us.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how the site is used so we can improve it.

Third-party services

To run the site we rely on a few trusted third-party providers, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Substack — hosts our newsletter. When you subscribe, your email is stored with Substack and is subject to their privacy policy.
  • Web3Forms — delivers our form submissions to us by email.
  • Google Analytics — provides the anonymized usage statistics described above.
  • Our hosting and connector infrastructure — serves the site and relays your newsletter sign-up to Substack.

We share information with these providers only to the extent needed to deliver the service you requested.

Cookies

The site itself does not set marketing cookies. Our analytics provider may set cookies or similar identifiers to measure aggregate usage. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time; the site will continue to work.

Your choices and rights

  • Unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link at the bottom of any newsletter email.
  • Access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you — just email us (below) and we will act on your request.
  • Opt out of analytics by using browser privacy settings or a tracker-blocking extension.

Data retention

We keep newsletter subscriptions until you unsubscribe, and messages you send us for as long as needed to respond and keep a reasonable record of our correspondence. We do not keep personal information longer than we need it.

Children

This site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page.
